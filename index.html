How to read this page

Cup score
The average of point skill and interval skill. Ties are broken by interval skill.
Point skill
1 − (your squared misses) / (the benchmark's), on the log scale: an out-of-sample R².
Interval skill
1 − (your interval scores) / (the benchmark's). You pay for width, and 20 times the miss when the truth lands outside.
Coverage
Share of public counties whose truth landed inside your 90% interval. Honest intervals land near 0.90.
Reference rows
The benchmark (constant mean) and an instructor reference built with tools from this course.

The response in the Regression Cup is synthetic: it was simulated from a secret model, using real county predictors from County Health Rankings & Roadmaps. It is not real EV data.
